Who I am

Since 2023 I've worked as an identity management and governance consultant, and I'm also an account manager: for the clients I own, I'm the main point of contact and I make the decisions about everything that happens in those accounts. My goal is easy to explain and hard to sustain: nobody should have more access than they need and, when an audit comes, everything should be provable.

I adapt to each client's platform: the NetIQ suite, Active Directory, Entra ID and One Identity's full Identity Fabric suite: Identity Manager (IGA), Safeguard (PAM), OneLogin (access management) and Active Roles (AD and Entra ID administration). But what I care about most is governance: defining who approves what, how access is reviewed and how to comply with ISO 27001 and NIST CSF without slowing the business down.

Outside work I'm studying for a B.Sc. in Cybersecurity, training in Blue Team and digital forensics with my own labs, building tools for my day-to-day work and writing on my blog. In 2026 I gave a talk at Ekoparty about identity governance.
